The Vision Behind Frame & Friend

I'm a filmmaking student in Indore who lives and breathes cinema. From Nolan's mathematical symmetry to Miyazaki's soul-stirring landscapes, from the lore of Dark Souls to the narrative depth of The Last of Us—I find stories everywhere.

I started Frame & Friend for the introverts and dreamers who want to look like the main character of their own story without the social awkwardness. For the people who understand that aesthetic isn't vanity—it's self-expression.

This isn't just photography. It's about creating moments that feel like they belong in a film. Every frame tells a story, and every story deserves to be captured with intention, artistry, and a deep understanding of visual language.

Safety & Trust

Stealth Mode

Your safety and comfort are paramount. Always.

We operate in complete transparency and professionalism. No bulky cameras, no awkward setups, no red flags. Just two people creating art in public spaces, with every safety measure in place.
